Bhai Dooj 2026 Date

Bhai Dooj 2026 falls on November 11, Wednesday, three days after Diwali. It is observed on Dwitiya Tithi of Kartik Shukla Paksha. Also known as Yama Dwitiya, it celebrates the bond between brothers and sisters.

Bhai Dooj Ceremony Vidhi

The sister prepares a puja thali with roli/kumkum, akshat, diya, sweets and a gift. She applies tilak on the brother's forehead, performs aarti, feeds him sweets and prays for his long life and prosperity. The brother offers gifts or money in return. Customs vary by region — some families include a full meal together.
