Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Date

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 date is September 14, 2026, Monday. It is observed during Shukla Paksha Chaturthi in the Hindu month of Bhadrapada. The Gregorian date is the same across the city references checked for this guide, while the exact Muhurat varies according to local sunrise and Panchang calculations.

For a broader festival overview, visit our Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 festival guide, which includes city-wise timing information and FAQs.

The tithi crossing midnight is why a simple calendar-date search can be confusing. The festival observance is on September 14, even though the Chaturthi lunar day continues into September 15.

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Puja Muhurat

Puja timing should not be treated as one India-wide clock time. For example, the cited 2026 Panchang reference gives New Delhi a Madhyahna Muhurat of 11:02 AM–1:31 PM, Mumbai 11:20 AM–1:48 PM, Pune 11:16 AM–1:44 PM and Ahmedabad 11:21 AM–1:49 PM. These are local times.

Use the site's Muhurat Finder and city-specific festival information before publishing or relying on a timing.

Ganesh Visarjan 2026

The cited Panchang reference lists Friday, September 25, 2026 as Ganesh Visarjan / Anant Chaturdashi. Local immersion schedules can differ because public processions, housing societies and local customs may choose different days.

How to Plan for Ganesh Chaturthi 2026

  • Confirm the local Sthapana Muhurat for your city.
  • Arrange the idol, puja samagri, flowers and prasad in advance.
  • Keep a clean puja space and plan the installation before the Muhurat window starts.
  • If possible, choose an eco-friendly clay idol and follow your local immersion rules.
  • For public celebrations, check local permissions and traffic or immersion guidance.
