Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Puja, Visarjan & Festival Planning

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 begins with the main festival day on September 14, Monday. This guide connects the date with Sthapana, daily puja, Visarjan planning and practical preparation.

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Puja Preparation

Once you know the Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 date, the next step is practical preparation. Keep the puja space clean, decide where the idol will be placed, arrange flowers and prasad, and confirm the local Sthapana Muhurat before the festival day.

  • Ganesha idol suitable for your household or community tradition
  • Puja thali and basic worship items
  • Flowers, durva grass and other customary offerings
  • Modak or another preferred prasad
  • Clean cloth and a stable platform for the idol

Ganesh Sthapana on September 14, 2026

The main Sthapana should be planned around the local Muhurat rather than a generic clock time. For example, the cited reference lists New Delhi at 11:02 AM–1:31 PM and Ahmedabad at 11:21 AM–1:49 PM. Your city may have another window.

For the main festival details and a larger city list, see the Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 guide.

Daily Puja During Ganeshotsav

Many families and communities continue worship through the festival period. The exact routine varies by tradition, so there is no need to present one universal ritual sequence as mandatory. A simple daily practice can include cleaning the puja space, offering flowers and prasad, performing aarti and maintaining a respectful devotional environment.

Ganesh Visarjan 2026

The cited 2026 Panchang reference lists Friday, September 25 for Ganesh Visarjan / Anant Chaturdashi. However, Ganesh idols are also commonly immersed on earlier days depending on family or community tradition. If you participate in a public immersion, follow the official local route, timing and environmental instructions.

Eco-friendly Ganesh Chaturthi Planning

  • Prefer a natural clay idol where appropriate.
  • Use reusable decoration materials instead of disposable plastic.
  • Keep offerings and waste separate during cleanup.
  • Use an authorized immersion location and follow local municipal guidance.
  • Plan transport and crowd safety for community events.
Local rules matter: Immersion arrangements can change by city, lake, river, housing society or municipal authority. Check the current local instructions close to the event date.
