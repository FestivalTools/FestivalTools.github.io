Diwali 2026 Date

Diwali 2026 falls on November 8, Sunday. It is observed on Amavasya Tithi of Kartik month. Amavasya begins at 11:27 AM on November 8 and ends at 12:31 PM on November 9, 2026.

Lakshmi Puja Vidhi

Clean and decorate the puja area. Place Lakshmi-Ganesha images or murtis on a red cloth. Arrange diyas, flowers (marigold/lotus), sweets, coins and panchameva. Light the diyas, offer flowers, perform aarti and chant Lakshmi mantra. Close with distribution of prasad. Customs vary by family tradition.

Diwali Safety Tips

Use diyas and candles safely — keep them away from children, curtains and combustible materials. If using fireworks, follow local civic rules and handle only in open areas. Eco-friendly diyas and reduced-smoke options are increasingly popular.
