Dussehra 2026 Date

Dussehra / Vijayadashami 2026 falls on October 20, Tuesday. Dashami Tithi begins at 12:50 PM on October 20 and ends at 02:11 PM on October 21. It marks the victory of Rama over Ravana and concludes the Navratri festival season.

Vijay Muhurat 2026 — City-wise

Vijay Muhurat is the afternoon Aparahna window considered most auspicious for worship, Shastra Puja and new ventures on Dussehra. It falls roughly 2–3 PM in most cities.

Ravan Dahan

Ravan Dahan — the symbolic burning of Ravana's effigy — typically takes place during the evening hours of Dussehra. Timing varies by city and local organizing committee. Check your local Ramlila committee or municipal event schedule for the specific time and venue.

Shastra Puja / Ayudha Puja

Many families, businesses and communities perform Shastra Puja (worship of tools, vehicles and instruments) on Dussehra as a symbol of seeking blessings for their work. It is particularly popular in South India as Ayudha Puja.
