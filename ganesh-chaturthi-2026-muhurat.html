If you searched for “Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 date” because you also need the right Sthapana time, the festival falls on September 14, 2026. The tithi spans two civil dates, while the recommended Madhyahna puja window changes by city.

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Tithi

The 2026 Chaturthi Tithi begins at 7:06 AM on September 14 and ends at 7:44 AM on September 15 in the cited Indian Panchang reference. This is the lunar timing behind the festival date. It is different from simply saying the festival lasts from midnight to midnight.

That distinction matters for SEO and for readers: a page targeting Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 date should answer the civil date first, then explain the lunar tithi and local Muhurat.

Why Does Ganesh Chaturthi Muhurat Differ by City?

Traditional Muhurat calculations can depend on local astronomical conditions, especially sunrise and the location used for the Panchang. That is why a Mumbai timing can be different from an Ahmedabad, Delhi or Kolkata timing even on the same festival date.

For the same reason, avoid using a generic “India Muhurat” label when the underlying calculation is city-specific. Clear location labels improve both user trust and the technical quality of a festival information page.

How to Use the Sthapana Muhurat

  1. Identify your exact city or the closest supported location.
  2. Confirm the festival date: September 14, 2026.
  3. Check the local Madhyahna / Sthapana window.
  4. Complete the installation and core puja within the recommended window where your tradition calls for it.
  5. For family or community observances, follow the Panchang and tradition you normally use.
