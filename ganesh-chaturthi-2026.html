Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Date

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 falls on September 14, Monday. Chaturthi Tithi begins at 07:06 AM on September 14 and ends at 07:44 AM on September 15. Anant Chaturdashi (Visarjan) is on September 24, 2026.

Ganesh Puja Vidhi

Clean and decorate the puja space. Install the Ganesha murti or idol on a raised platform. Offer durva grass, red flowers, modak, coconut and panchameva. Perform Ganesh Sthapana puja, aarti and chant Ganesh mantra. Keep the murti for 1.5, 5 or 10 days according to family tradition before Visarjan.

Ganesh Visarjan 2026

Visarjan (immersion) can be done on Day 1.5 (Sep 15), Day 5 (Sep 18), Day 7 (Sep 20) or Day 10 — Anant Chaturdashi on September 24, 2026. Follow environmental guidelines and use eco-friendly idols where possible.
