About FestivalTools

FestivalTools is designed as a lightweight information hub for Indian festival dates and practical tools. Exact astronomical timings such as Muhurat, Tithi and Bhadra are intentionally separated from general festival dates so that verified Panchang data can be added without inventing values. All city-specific timings shown on this site are sourced from cited Panchang references.
