Janmashtami 2026 Date

Krishna Janmashtami 2026 falls on September 4, Friday. It is observed on Krishna Paksha Ashtami of Bhadrapada month. Ashtami Tithi begins at 02:25 AM on September 4 and ends at 12:13 AM on September 5, 2026.

Fasting and Parana

Devotees observe a full-day fast on Janmashtami and break it (Parana) after completing midnight puja on September 5, once Ashtami Tithi ends. Exact Parana timing for your city — verify with local Panchang. Some traditions allow Parana at sunrise on Sep 5.

Janmashtami Puja Vidhi

Clean and decorate the puja space. Set up a swing (jhula) for the Krishna idol. Offer flowers, tulsi leaves, panchamrit, butter and sweets. Perform aarti at Nishita Kaal, sing bhajans and kirtans. At midnight, celebrate Lord Krishna's birth by rocking the cradle and distributing prasad.
