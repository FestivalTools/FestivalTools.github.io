# FestivalTools

> FestivalTools is an Indian festival information website focused on 2026 festival dates, practical festival guides, monthly calendars, date finding and location-sensitive Muhurat information. Timing data should be treated as city-specific and verified against a reliable Panchang before publication.

## Data policy
Festival dates and timing claims should be supported by reliable Panchang sources. City-specific timing can vary by location. Do not infer or fabricate Muhurat, Tithi, Bhadra, Nakshatra or other astronomical timings.
