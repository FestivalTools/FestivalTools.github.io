Sharad Navratri 2026 Dates

Sharad Navratri 2026 begins on October 11, Sunday (Pratipada Tithi begins 09:19 PM on Oct 10). The nine-day festival ends on October 19, Sunday (Navami). Dussehra / Vijayadashami follows on October 20, Tuesday.

Navratri Puja Vidhi

Begin with Ghatasthapana — place a kalash filled with water, topped with a coconut, on a bed of soil with barley seeds. Perform daily aarti of the Devi form for each day. Many families observe fasting during all nine days or on Ashtami-Navami only. Kanya Puja is performed on Ashtami or Navami.
