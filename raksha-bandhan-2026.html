Raksha Bandhan 2026 Date

Raksha Bandhan 2026 falls on August 28, Friday. It is observed on Purnima Tithi of Shravan month. Purnima begins at 09:08 AM on August 27 and ends at 09:48 AM on August 28, 2026.

Rakhi Tying Muhurat 2026 — City-wise

The Rakhi tying window opens after sunrise (after Bhadra ends) and remains open until Purnima Tithi ends at 09:48 AM. Bhadra ends before sunrise on August 28 in most cities, so the full morning is auspicious.

Bhadra Kaal and Purnima Tithi

Traditionally Rakhi should not be tied during Bhadra Kaal. On August 28, 2026, Bhadra ends before sunrise in most cities — making the entire morning auspicious for Rakhi tying. Purnima Tithi ends at 09:48 AM. After that, Rakhi can still be tied throughout the day as a family observance.

Raksha Bandhan Puja Vidhi

Prepare a thali with Rakhi, roli or kumkum, akshat, diya and sweets. Apply tilak on the brother's forehead, perform aarti, tie the Rakhi on the right wrist and offer sweets. Exchange gifts or blessings. Customs vary by family and regional tradition.
